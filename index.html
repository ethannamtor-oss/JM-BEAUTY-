Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before placing your order.

How do I place an order?

Simply click the "Order" button on any product, or click the WhatsApp icon at the bottom right. It will open a chat with us directly. We handle all orders personally via WhatsApp.

Are your perfumes authentic?

Yes, 100%. We source our designer perfumes directly from authorized distributors, and our niche/extrait blends are crafted with premium, high-concentration oils.

Do you deliver nationwide?

Yes, we deliver to all states in Nigeria. Delivery within Lagos takes 24-48 hours, while other states take 2-4 working days. Payment is required before dispatch.

Can I buy sample sizes first?

Absolutely. We highly recommend our Discovery Sample Set. It's the best way to test the longevity and scent profile on your skin before investing in a full bottle.
